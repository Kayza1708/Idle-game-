# Active Gameplay + Late Hardware Pass

- Late hardware costs now scale from e14 to e125 instead of ending around e19.
- Taps use ScientificNumber and scale with quality, hardware milestones, research, prestige, items and combo.
- Deterministic critical tap every ~20 taps, x3 base.
- New repeatable research: Impulsverstärkung and Signalprotokolle.
- Item profiles can roll tap/drop effects through existing rarity secondary/tertiary system.
- Active signal drops scale in spawn frequency and rarity with research/items/prestige.
- Drops live 8 seconds and appear as moving coin/cache/core targets over the game UI.
- Cache/core drops grant 2x/3x component quantity.
